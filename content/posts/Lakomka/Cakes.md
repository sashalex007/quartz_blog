## **Honey Cake (Medovik): A Classic with Natural Sweetness**  

**Honey cake (medovik)** is a well-loved dessert in Eastern Europe, known for its **thin layers of honey-infused dough** and creamy filling. As the cake rests, the layers absorb the filling, creating a **soft, melt-in-your-mouth texture**.  

### **What Makes Honey Cake Special?**  
- **Natural honey** gives it a rich, caramel-like sweetness.  
- The layers soften over time, making it even more flavourful.  
- It pairs beautifully with tea or coffee for a comforting treat.  

Honey cake is a wonderful balance of sweetness and texture, making it a favourite for both special occasions and everyday enjoyment.  

## **Napoleon (Cremesh): Crisp Layers and Creamy Filling**  

**Napoleon cake (also known as cremesh)** is a perfect combination of **flaky pastry and smooth custard filling**. Inspired by the French **mille-feuille**, this cake is made with **thin, buttery puff pastry sheets** layered with creamy custard or pastry cream.  

### **Why Napoleon Cake Stands Out**  
- The **buttery, crisp pastry** contrasts beautifully with the soft filling.  
- The **custard or pastry cream** is rich but not too heavy.  
- As the cake rests, the layers soften slightly, creating a perfect balance of textures.  

This cake is both elegant and comforting, making it a great choice for any occasion.  

## **Marlenka: A Modern Take on a Traditional Favourite**  

Inspired by honey cake, **Marlenka** is a variation that combines the natural sweetness of honey with a moist, tender texture. Made with **simple, natural ingredients**, Marlenka cakes have a deep, caramelized flavour and a long shelf life without preservatives.  

### **Our Selection of Marlenka Cakes**  
- **Classic Marlenka** – A traditional honey cake with **layers of honey-infused dough and a creamy filling**.  
- **Chocolate Marlenka** – A richer version with **cocoa-infused layers** for a deeper, more indulgent flavour.  
- **Gluten-Free Marlenka** – The same delicious honey cake, made without gluten so that everyone can enjoy it.  

Marlenka cakes are a great option for those who appreciate **natural ingredients and classic flavours**.  

## **Cheesecakes: Smooth, Creamy, and Timeless**  

Cheesecake is loved for its **rich, velvety texture and perfect balance of sweetness and tanginess**. Made with fresh cream cheese, eggs, and a carefully baked crust, it’s a dessert that feels both simple and indulgent.  

### **Classic Cheesecake**  
A smooth, creamy filling on a buttery graham cracker crust, **classic cheesecake** is a favourite for its rich yet light taste. Whether enjoyed plain or topped with fresh fruit, it’s always a satisfying choice.  

### **European-Style Cheesecake**  
Lighter than the traditional New York-style cheesecake, **European-style cheesecakes** often use farmer’s cheese or ricotta, giving them a slightly tangy flavour. These cheesecakes are less sweet but just as delicious, making them a great option for those who enjoy a more delicate dessert.
