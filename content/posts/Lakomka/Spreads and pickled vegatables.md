
Eastern European cuisine is known for its **rich, savoury spreads and tangy pickled vegetables**, adding depth and flavour to everyday meals. From the **smoky sweetness of ajvar** to the **tangy crunch of sour cabbage**, these traditional favourites are perfect for **sandwiches, side dishes, or simply enjoying with fresh bread**.  

## **Spreads: A Taste of Roasted Vegetables and Bold Spices**  

### **Eggplant Spread, Ratatouille & Zucchini Spread**  
These **vegetable-based spreads** are smooth, rich, and full of flavour. Whether made with **roasted eggplant, zucchini, or a mix of garden vegetables**, they are perfect for **spreading on bread, serving as a dip, or pairing with grilled meats**.  

### **Luteniza, Ajvar & Zacusca**  
These classic Balkan and Eastern European spreads are made from **roasted red peppers, tomatoes, and eggplant**, each with its own regional twist:  
- **Luteniza** – A Bulgarian favourite, slightly chunky and mildly spiced.  
- **Ajvar** – A Serbian and Macedonian spread, smooth and smoky with a hint of sweetness.  
- **Zacusca** – A Romanian specialty, featuring roasted vegetables with a rich, deep flavour.  

### **Adjika: A Spicy Kick**  
A **fiery Georgian and Russian spread**, adjika is made from **hot peppers, garlic, and herbs**, adding a bold, savoury heat to meats, sandwiches, or soups.  

## **Pickled Vegetables: A Perfect Balance of Tangy and Savoury**  

### **Pickled & Roasted Red Peppers**  
Pickled red peppers offer a **sweet and tangy crunch**, while roasted red peppers have a **smoky depth**, making both a great addition to **salads, sandwiches, or charcuterie boards**.  

### **Sour Cabbage & Pickled Tomatoes**  
- **Sour cabbage** is a staple in **Eastern European cuisine**, used for **stuffed cabbage rolls, salads, or as a side dish**.  
- **Pickled tomatoes** bring a **unique, slightly tangy flavour**, perfect for **snacking or pairing with meats and cheeses**.  

## **Mustards, Horseradish & Zakucon: Bold Condiments**  

### **Eastern European Mustards & Horseradish**  
Mustards from **Romania, Ukraine, and Poland** range from **mild and creamy to sharp and spicy**, adding depth to sandwiches and meats. Horseradish is a **classic pairing for sausages, roasts, and cold cuts**, offering a sharp, pungent kick.  

### **Zakucon: A Traditional Relish**  
A **flavourful mix of pickled vegetables and spices**, zakucon is a great addition to grilled meats or as a tangy side dish.
