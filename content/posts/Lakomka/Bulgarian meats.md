
Bulgarian cured meats are known for their **bold spices, careful aging, and deep flavours**, reflecting centuries of Balkan culinary tradition. Whether you prefer the **intense seasoning of pasturma, the delicate texture of File Elena, or the rich depth of lukanka**, these meats bring a **distinct taste of Bulgaria** to any meal.  

## **Pasturma: Bold and Aromatic**  
Pasturma is a **dry-cured beef or pork** coated in a fragrant spice blend of **fenugreek, garlic, and black pepper**. It has a **firm texture and intense flavour**, making it perfect for **thin slicing and serving with bread, cheese, and wine**.  

## **File Elena: Delicate and Refined**  
File Elena is a **lean, air-dried pork loin**, lightly salted and aged for a **mild yet savoury flavour**. It’s often enjoyed as part of a **mezze platter** with cheese, olives, and fresh vegetables.  

## **Lukanka: Bulgaria’s Signature Dry Sausage**  
Lukanka is a **semi-dry cured sausage** made from **pork and beef**, seasoned with **black pepper and cumin**, and pressed into a **distinct flattened shape**. It has a **slightly tangy, rich taste** and is best served **thinly sliced with fresh bread and rakia (Bulgarian brandy)**.  

## **Sudjuk: Spiced and Savoury**  
Sudjuk is a **bold, peppery dry sausage** made from **beef or pork**, heavily seasoned with **garlic, cumin, and black pepper**. It’s great for **snacking, pan-frying with eggs, or adding to stews**.  

## **Bulgarian Salamis**  
Bulgarian salamis come in a variety of styles, from **mild and smoky to spicy and peppery**. They are perfect for **sandwiches, charcuterie boards, or pairing with wine and cheese**.
