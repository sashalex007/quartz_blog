## **Carrot Salad: A Simple Yet Flavourful Classic**  

Our **carrot salad** is a perfect balance of **sweetness and tang**, made with **freshly grated carrots, a touch of seasoning, and a light dressing**. It’s a refreshing side that pairs well with **grilled meats, sandwiches, or as part of a larger meal**.  

## **Kapusta: Traditional Cabbage Salad**  

Kapusta, or **cabbage salad**, is a staple in **Eastern European cuisine**, known for its **crisp texture and tangy, savoury flavour**. Made with **fresh cabbage, carrots, and a perfectly balanced dressing**, our kapusta is a **light yet satisfying dish** that complements just about any meal.  

## **Shuba: A Layered Favourite**  

Also known as **"herring under a fur coat,"** shuba is a **beautifully layered salad** featuring **pickled herring, potatoes, beets, carrots, and eggs**, all brought together with a **creamy dressing**. This rich and flavourful dish is a **must-try for anyone who loves traditional Slavic cuisine**.  

## **Seaweed Salad: A Unique and Nutritious Option**  

For those looking for something a little different, our **seaweed salad** offers a **savoury, umami-rich flavour** packed with **vitamins and minerals**. Lightly seasoned and perfectly balanced, it’s a great choice for a **nutritious side dish or a refreshing appetizer**.
